# Admin SPA readability

Read before changing what a screen shows, in any state (placeholder, error, empty, loaded).

---

## Admin SPA readability

Text must be readable, not only present, and the staff SPA is read on a phone at the door as often as at a desk. A screen that is waiting, has failed or is empty is checked like a full one: before a PR changes what a screen shows, look at it in real Chrome at 1280px and at 390px, in each state the loading standard above describes (the placeholder, the slow note, the error with **Retry**, the loaded page), with data in it that is as long as real data is (an address as a name, a note with a long word, a row with a time and a person) and in every tab of the page, not only the first one (the attendee page's Activity log and Notes went through this check empty, and on a phone a row left its text a column of 66px and the time of a note stuck out of the card), and fix these faults:

- **A lone word, or a column of one or two words.** A block of text must not end on a single word ("connection." alone on the second line of a message), and must not be so narrow that a line holds one or two words. Every text inherits `text-wrap: balance` from `body` (`packages/ui/src/styles/tokens/base.css`; a block of up to six lines gets even lines, a longer one wraps as before; `text-wrap: pretty` was tried and does not do this), so a new text is covered without a rule of its own: do not switch it off. Do not give a text a column narrower than about 16 characters either: a control that stands beside text (a notice's action, a header's buttons, the toggles of a table) goes under the text on a phone instead of squeezing it (`.at-notice--has-action`, the notification matrix in `notifications-panel.css`, `.attendee-detail-pageheader`, the rows of the Activity log in `attendees.css`).
- **A word cut off.** A label, a name or an address is never cut to "Attende…" or "e2e.admin@ex…". Do not put `white-space: nowrap` with `text-overflow: ellipsis` on text that has no other place to be read (a table cell on a desktop whose `title` has the whole text is the one exception): let it wrap and let its box grow. A tile or a row that holds text has a `min-height`, never a fixed `height` (`.overview-kpi`, `.users-page__stat`, `.reports-stat`), and `overflow: hidden` is a backstop for a single word that cannot fit, not the layout. Text that an ancestor with `overflow: hidden` cuts off without an ellipsis is the same fault.
- **A word broken in the middle.** `word-break: break-all` turned "Internal network" into "Interna" / "l network": use `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, which breaks inside a word only when it does not fit. A hyphenated word may break at its hyphen, which is ordinary typography; one that leaves one or two letters on the next line ("check-" / "in") means the column is too narrow.
- **A table that squeezes its cells.** A table whose cells take four or five lines each (the Active sessions table at 1280px: the login time and the signer's local time, the address, 117px a row) is too wide for its columns: drop the column that matters least for what the table is for at that width, never squeeze every cell. The sessions table has a ladder (cards up to 768px; from 769px to 1180px Device, IP address and Sign-in drop, `.sessions-col-tablet-hide`; from 1181px to 1499px only Sign-in, `.sessions-col-laptop-hide`; all eight columns from 1500px): what an admin reviewing sessions acts on (who, from where, when, and the revoke) stays as long as there is room, and the sign-in method is the first to go (the Account page's own table drops it first too). Widening a column is not the fix: it pushed the Revoke icon out of the table.

What the pages were checked with is a script that walks the text of each state in real Chrome and measures it: the box of each word, so the lines, the words of the last line, a word split across two lines, a block under 16 characters a line, `text-overflow: ellipsis` that is cut, and text outside an ancestor with `overflow: hidden`. It is not part of the repository (it needs a running instance with data), so the rules above are what it looks for. `apps/admin/test/styles/readable-text-css.test.ts` pins the CSS that these rules rest on, which the tests in jsdom cannot see.
