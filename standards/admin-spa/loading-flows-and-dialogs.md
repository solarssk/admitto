# Loading recipes: step flows, dialogs and lookups

Read when you touch the send panels, Add attendee, Import, the setup wizard, the custom field editor or any dialog that offers options from its own lookup. The shared rules are in [loading.md](loading.md).

---

### Panels with two independent actions (send panels)

- A panel with two buttons that start independent actions (Count recipients and Send in the two send panels) has one busy flag for each: each button is `loading` for its own action (`loadingLabel` only when it is not longer than the label at rest), and the other is `disabled`, which is right because it is not the one that holds the focus. The control that holds the keyboard focus goes away with the step it started (Send, when the progress replaces the form; Stop, when the result replaces it; Send another, when the form replaces it), so those three report that they hold the focus (`useFocusHandover`, `onFocus`) and the focus moves on to the next step's first control (Stop, the status that says a Wallets message is on its way, Send another, the checked recipient card) once the step has changed, only when it has really fallen to the page by then, so an operator who has moved on is left alone. Nothing else in the panel is a step: a recovery that took any control that goes away (`useFocusRecovery` on the panel) would throw the focus to the top of the panel when an attendee chip is removed or a list closes. The create and edit dialogs of a template follow the plain rule: **Create** and **Save** are `Button loading` with the label they have at rest, the dialog that edits one tells Save from Delete by what the operator started (`pending`, since the page's busy flag covers both), and a field that Enter submits goes `readOnly` while it works, since a `disabled` field loses the focus.

### Add attendee dialog

- The Add attendee dialog (`AddAttendeeModal`) reads the ticket types and the custom fields as it opens, and the form appears as one piece once both are in: its fields are in the dialog but invisible, so it has its size, with `FieldsSkeleton` drawn over them after 200ms and kept for at least 400ms. Each read has the 30 second limit of its own and ends in an error with a Retry that reruns only that read. The placeholder has one status region, `add-attendee-modal__loading-status` (a visually hidden "Loading attendee form" and, after 8 seconds of the first wait, the shared `SlowNote`), which sits between the fields and the buttons and not in the skeleton, because the skeleton has exactly the size of the fields and cannot make room for a line. The 8 seconds are counted again for each opening and each event (`useDelayedLoading(open && firstLoad, SLOW_NOTICE_MS, eventId)`: a close that left a read unfinished must not keep the old count running). The check-in history works the same way: the placeholder of `CkRecentScans`, in the sidebar (`ScanHistoryList`) and in the phone camera view (`CameraOverlay`), has a status region (`ck-recent__loading-status`: "Loading recent scans" and the note after 8 seconds), and on the dark overlay the note takes the light text of its neighbours (`.ck-overlay__aside .slow-note`). A failed first load of the history is a `Notice` with a busy Retry (`ScanHistoryError`), and where the keyboard focus goes when that Retry works depends on the screen: the page puts it on the scan field (`CheckInPage`, when the status goes from error to ready and the focus has fallen to `<body>`, straight away and not through `focusScan`, which waits for an animation frame and would pull back an operator who moved on in it), because a keyboard-wedge scanner types into whatever holds the focus and a focus that fell to the page would swallow the next scan; the phone camera view has no scan field, so it hands the focus to its list (`landmark=".ck-overlay__aside"`, `useRetryFocusHandover`).

### Import: a flow of steps with a long job

- A page that is a flow of steps with a long job (Import: choose a file, Validate, Commit) has one busy flag for each action (`validating`, `committing`) and each shows it on its own button (`Button loading`: **Validate file** and **Re-validate** say "Validating…", **Commit import** says "Importing…", none longer than its label at rest), while what cannot be used meanwhile and is not the busy control (Re-validate while Commit runs, Commit while Re-validate runs, the switches, the remove-file chip) stays `disabled`. Neither action reports progress (the file goes to the server, and the commit is a job that is polled), so the card of the file runs an indeterminate `TopProgressBar placement="container"` from the click, named for what happens ("Validating file", "Importing attendees"), with no 200ms hold: the operator has just asked for it. The Validate button is replaced by the summary and the Commit button by the done step, so both report that they hold the focus (`useFocusHandover`) and it moves to the summary's title or the done step's title (a `span` or a heading with `tabindex="-1"`), and from **Import another file** to the drop zone, only when it has really fallen to the page. The page is a body keyed by the route's id (`ImportPage` renders `ImportPageBody key={eventId}`): another event is a fresh page, without the file, the summary or the history of the previous one, and a validation or a commit that was still on its way is aborted with the old body (`previewImport` takes a signal), so it never toasts, or fills a summary, on the new page. The history is a card list (`useListLoad` and `useCardLoad` in `ImportHistoryCard`: `ImportHistorySkeleton` with the real column headings, measured in Chrome, a `RetryEmptyState`, a `RefetchRegion` for the refresh that follows an import, which keeps the rows, and a `RefreshWarning` when that refresh fails), and the event's custom columns, a lookup of their own, are `useOptionsLoad` with `CustomColumnsStatus` (a placeholder, and a `RetryHint` when it failed: a table without the event's own columns must not pass for the whole list).

### Setup wizard: one footer for every step

- A wizard whose footer drives every step (the setup wizard) has one footer for all of them, so the button that holds the keyboard focus when the wizard moves on is the same element on the next step (Continue becomes Open dashboard, Back stays Back) and keeps it, and its buttons follow the plain rules. **Save & Continue** is `Button loading` ("Saving…"), and step 4's **Continue** has no busy label, because "Creating…" would be longer than "Continue" and make the button wider all the time: its spinner covers the label, and a visually hidden status (`workStatus`) says what is happening. **Open dashboard** is `Button loading` ("Finishing…"). **Back** and **Skip for now** are `disabled` while the step is being saved, since they are not the busy control (Back used to stay active and could take the wizard back in the middle of a save). **Continue** goes off for a reason of the step (the system checks have not passed, the first event's form is empty) with `aria-disabled`, never `disabled`, because the button that has just moved the wizard on turns itself off on the same commit when the next step is not ready. The test-send button of the mail step ("Sending…"), **Create event** in the New event dialog ("Creating…") and **Continue** of the device label step ("Saving…") are plain `Button loading`, and the device label field, which Enter submits, goes `readOnly` while it saves, since a `disabled` field loses the focus.

### Editor whose Save depends on a lookup (custom field editor)

- An editor whose Save depends on a lookup of its own (the custom field editor on Requirements: how many attendees hold each option of a choice field, which decides whether a rename or a removal is risky) reads it through `useOptionUsage` (on `useOptionsLoad`, read when the editor opens, with the 30 second limit) and never turns it into a label of the Save button ("Checking usage…" is a busy-label swap): each row's count is a bar in the real pill's size (`OptionsEditor` `usageLoading`, held invisibly for the first 200ms and kept for at least 400ms once drawn, so what the row says is gated on `showContent` and not only on the counts being in, and one always-mounted status region says that the counts are being read once the bars are drawn (visually hidden, and not for an answer that comes at once) and, after 8 seconds, shows the shared note "Taking longer than usual" (`SLOW_NOTICE_TEXT` from `useDelayedLoading(usageLoading, SLOW_NOTICE_MS)`, a visible line above the rows) in that same region, so that it is announced too), a failed read is a `RetryHint` (busy, kept on screen, announced again) with every count saying "Unknown", never "Unused", and Save and the remove buttons stay off while the counts are not known. Save (and Create field) is `Button loading` with the label it has at rest, and the fields that Enter submits (the label, the description, the option fields) are `readOnly` while it works, not `disabled`, which would drop the focus of an operator who pressed Enter in one of them; the submit handler ignores a second submit while it works (Enter does not go through the button, which swallows a click). A row of the System status menu that is still being checked ends in the kit's `Spinner` (`aria-hidden`, since the row's own text says "Checking…"), not an icon glyph that is spun by hand. The menu's trigger never says "All systems normal" before every row has answered: while no row has failed and some are still being checked it says "Checking systems…" with a neutral dot (`worstRowState` returns `pending`; a failure that is already known is shown at once), held invisibly for the first 200ms and kept for at least 400ms once drawn (`useLoadingGate`). The reads behind it that somebody waits for have the 30 second limit (`loadWithTimeout`: the first read of the checks and a Retry of it, and the first read of an event's mail transport; a tick of the 30 second poll has none): checks that do not answer are rows saying "Unavailable" with a **Check again** item in the menu (its Retry: a `role="menuitem"`, so that the arrow keys, Home and End reach it, busy like `<Button loading>` with `aria-disabled` and a spinner, and kept on screen until the answer is in), and an always-mounted visually hidden `role="alert"` says that they did not answer (the panel exists only while it is open), as a new element again when a retry ends with the failure still there; the next tick asks again by itself as well. The reads overlap (a tick falls due while a retry is on its way), so they are numbered (`pollRead`): an older answer never replaces a newer one, and a failure of a read somebody waits for is not reported when an answer has been applied while it ran. A superadmin in an event has the Email sending row being checked until that event's own answer is in, since the organization's status is only the fallback for a read that failed or ran out of time, never a stand-in for one on its way.

### Options a dialog offers from a lookup

- The options a dialog offers from a lookup of their own (the events, the organizations of a scope picker, a short list such as Recent logins) load through `useOptionsLoad` (`apps/admin/src/hooks/useOptionsLoad.ts`; `useEventOptions`, `useOrganizationOptions`, `useRecentLogins` are its uses), read while the dialog is open, each with the 30 second limit: the field's place is a `LookupSlot` (a placeholder of the field's size, invisible for the first 200ms, and saying "Taking longer than usual" with `SlowNote` once `useOptionsLoad` reports the lookup `slow`, after 8 seconds counted from the request on screen (a lookup that replaces one still on its way starts its 8 seconds again); the other placeholders of a lookup, Recent logins and the import's custom columns, do the same), a failure is a one-line `RetryHint` that reruns that lookup only, and a failed lookup is never an empty list ("No organizations available", "No recent logins" are for an answer that says so). What a lookup names (a scope chip) shows a placeholder, or the kind of thing ("Event"), while the lookup is on its way or failed (and the chip's remove button is off then, since chips of a kind look alike); an id is only the fallback for a scope that an answer does not hold. A lookup shows nothing of an earlier answer once it starts again or fails (`useOptionsLoad` clears its items), and when several Retry hints can be on screen each Retry has its own accessible name (`RetryHint retryLabel`). The action a confirmation runs says its failure inside the `ConfirmDialog` (`errorMessage`), which sits above the dialog that opened it: a message on the dialog behind is hidden by the confirmation's backdrop. A save that went through in part (the profile, but not every role change) keeps the dialog open for a retry and lets the list hear of what was saved when the dialog closes, because telling it sooner would hand the dialog a new person and reset what is still staged. The ticket types that the "By ticket type" recipient filter of the Communication and Wallets send panels offers are such a lookup too (`useTicketTypeOptions`, read as soon as the panel is there, so the field is rarely a placeholder by the time the filter is chosen), and while that lookup failed the field is there but off.

### Setup wizard: steps that read their data

- The steps of the setup wizard read their data when they open, and each first read follows the panel rules inside the body of the step (`pages/wizard/WizardStepLoad.tsx`): `WizardStepPlaceholder` is the status region around the shapes of the step (named after what loads, invisible for the first 200ms, with the 8 second note), and `WizardRetryNotice` is the error with a Retry that is busy while it works and hands the focus to `.setup-wizard__body` when it worked (`useRetryFocusHandover`), a body that the page names after the step (a `<section aria-label>`, which is a region) so that the hand-over is announced; the one-line `RetryHint` of step 4's lookup does the same through its optional `landmark` (a hint that has no region to hand the focus to, such as one in a dialog, passes none, and gets no hand-over). What replaces the placeholder of a step (the checks, the mail form, the branding form) fades in over 150ms (`at-fade-in` on the root of the ready content, as the settings panels do). The system checks of step 1 are a `useListLoad` (rows with the real labels and a skeleton for each icon and result, measured against the real rows in Chrome: 196px against 196px; a re-run keeps the results in a `RefetchRegion`, and a run that fails keeps them and says so in the one notice that holds the Retry (a `RefreshWarning` beside it would be a second Retry that runs the same checks, busy independently of the first)), and a read that failed is never a list of checks, so the step is not ready then. The mail and branding steps are `usePanelLoad`, and a step whose form a read fills in shows no form until that read has answered: a form that looks empty because the read failed is a form whose save overwrites what is stored (step 3 used to show a toast and the empty form, and a Save & Continue on it cleared the logo), so the step's save does nothing before the form is there. The lookup of the events that exist already in step 4 is a `useOptionsLoad` of its own: the form is there from the first frame, and a failure is a `RetryHint` that reruns only that lookup (until it works the wizard behaves as with no events, because nothing says there are some).
